Who we are

About Graceland

Graceland The Full Gospel Ministry is committed to raising men and women of excellence—grounded in the Word of God, growing through prayer and worship, and equipped to serve God and their community.

Through fellowship, teaching and Christian service, we encourage every person to grow in faith and live out the purpose God has given them.
